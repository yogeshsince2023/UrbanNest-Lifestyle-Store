# Future Improvements & Roadmap

While UrbanNest is fully featured and ready for immediate deployment as an artisanal showcase, the following extensions offer high-value next steps for production scaling:

---

## 1. Direct Payment Gateway Integration
- **Stripe & Razorpay Embedded Checkout**: Transition the current *"Order Inquiry"* flow into a direct instant-pay checkout supporting Apple Pay, Google Pay, credit cards, and UPI payments for standardized inventory items while retaining custom inquiry for bespoke goods.

## 2. Dynamic CMS & Studio Inventory Management
- **Headless CMS Connection (Sanity / Strapi / Supabase)**: Allow studio artisans to upload new glaze drops, update inventory counts, and publish seasonal blog posts without committing code changes.

## 3. Real-Time Webhook Cart Synchronization
- **Abandoned Parcel Recovery**: Send partial cart state to N8N on customer email entry to trigger personalized follow-ups for high-value curations.

## 4. Patron Accounts & Bespoke Wishlists
- **Saved Gift Lists**: Enable patrons to create curated wedding, housewarming, or holiday parcel registries with unique shareable links.

## 5. Multi-Currency & International Localization
- **Automated Geolocation Currency Switching**: Dynamic currency conversion (USD, EUR, GBP, INR) with localized shipping estimates based on visitor IP address.
